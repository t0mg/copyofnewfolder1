# CopyOfNewFolder's recs archive 2026

## TR-49
I played TR-49 this weekend. It's a deduction puzzle game about rebuilding a library index from the curator notes of its entries. Along the way you get to learn about the lives and relationships of the authors contained in the corpus, as well as its curators. I found the challenge of putting together a story that's presented out of order fun. If you like detective games and are willing to engage your text comprehension skills in particular, TR-49 is a good time for 5-6 hours.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/3838370/TR49/

## Grapple Dog
Grapple Dog is 2d platformer in the mold of Mario about an anthropomorphic dog with freakishly large hands, who finds a grappling hook and has to save the world from a robot invasion. This game is comfort food: a competent platformer with a grappling hook that gets tricky, but remains fair. It has an appealing visual style, the controls are responsive and satisfying, I liked how levels are presented both as collection challenges and time trials, and its boss battles are well designed and are there to test your core platforming skills. It won't break any expectations, it's all well done. My only complaints are that world 1 is a bit too easy and can set the wrong expectations, and that the game's music quickly starts to grate and should be replaced with something else. But minor nitpicks aside, it took me about 14 hours to 100% and I would recommend this if you like 2d platformers.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/1345860/Grapple_Dog/

## Timespinner
I've been playing Timespinner. Mechanically this is a 2d platformer metroidvania in the mold of Castlevania: Symphony of the Night and its derivatives, but with extra time travel flavor. The controls feel great, it has a good selection of abilities and environments, the map is reasonably sized and fun to uncover, and the pixel art, creature designs and music are all excellent.

What I liked less is the 2010s-era microagression-aware Young Adult fantasy novel writing. The characters are a range of genders and sexualities, universally accepting and accommodating, there's no conflict outside of the bad guy, and the protagonist's character arc is the typical stepping up to face the challenge, and it all plays out in predictable and somewhat boring way. It's never bad, but I felt that it was forgettable. I also wish the game offered more pushback in terms of difficulty, as it's too easy to overlevel your character and trivialize the combat, but you could also read it as more relaxed baseline difficulty for normal people.

Overall, Timespinner is a good metroidvania that follows the style of the classics with a forgettable story and a focus on a more chill experience. I 100%-ed it in about 12 hours and I think it's worth playing if you want to unwind or if you're looking for a PS1-era nostalgia hit.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/368620/Timespinner/
