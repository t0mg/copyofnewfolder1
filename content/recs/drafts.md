## BRAZILIAN DRUG DEALER 3: I OPENED A PORTAL TO HELL IN THE FAVELA TRYING TO REVIVE MIT AIA I NEED TO CLOSE IT 
BRAZILIAN DRUG DEALER 3: I OPENED A PORTAL TO HELL IN THE FAVELA TRYING TO REVIVE MIT AIA I NEED TO CLOSE IT is an unapologetically Brazilian FPS that celebrates their culture and is full incomprehensible in-jokes and memes. If you enjoy really stupid absurd humor or enthusiastic outsider art that can't be replicated by genAI, this is a very fun 3 hour experience.

## Cartomancy Anthology
Cartomancy Anthology is a free collection of games from different developers, each of which has been inspired by a different tarot card. Each one lasts about 10-30 minutes, I sampled about half of them and found some worthwhile experiments. It's well worth trying if you're looking for a palate cleanser or are short on time.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/1869870/Cartomancy_Anthology/

## Parking Garage Rally Circuit 
Parking Garage Rally Circuit is a very Sega-inspired racing game about getting the best lap time in various parking garages. The controls are simple but fun to master, you only have acceleration/brakes and Mario Kart-style drifts that give you speed. The game itself consists of 8 tracks (+8 more with DLC) and 3 vehicle classes, colorful visuals and an upbeat ska soundtrack. It's pure, dumb fun, but there isn't much game outside of chasing leaderboard positions, and it took me about 3.5 hours to get gold medals in all events of the base game. Would recommend this as a brief, uplifting palate cleanser.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/2737300/Parking_Garage_Rally_Circuit/

## HELENA: Cloud District 
HELENA: Cloud District is a short, free walking simulator about finding and photographing invasive orbs in the titular floating structure. The game has a nice atmosphere, with a warm color palette and friendly architecture, with lots of curves and blobby organic shapes. It reminds me a bit of studio Ghibli's work a bit in terms of building design and emotional tone. Another interesting detail is that the game doesn't have a dedicated jump button, instead you accelerate up near-vertical walls to gain height and cross large gaps. There is a story, but I didn't particularly care about its attempts at poetry. It takes about 1.5 hours to finish, would recommend if you're in a mood for some fantastical exploration.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/5190580/HELENA_Cloud_District/
